Crateshift — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Babar Imran (Bravo's Games). Contact: Papajohn3432@gmail.com

1. What the game stores on your device

Crateshift saves your progress (levels, stars, coins, endless-mode best stages), your settings and your purchase entitlements (for example Remove Ads) in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

2. Optional account

You can play without an account. If you choose to create an account or sign in with email or Google, authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. In that case we and Firebase process your email address and an account identifier. We do not receive your Google password. We use this only to sign you in and do not sell it.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, your IP address, device and app information and ad interactions. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. You can remove ads with the Remove Ads purchase in the Shop. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

5. Children

Crateshift is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Deleting your data

Game data stored on the device is deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. To delete your account and the email address and identifier stored with it, email Papajohn3432@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
